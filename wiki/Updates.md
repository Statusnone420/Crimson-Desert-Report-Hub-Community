# Updates

Follow [the current edition](https://crimsonreporthub.com/) and [News](https://crimsonreporthub.com/news) for new reporting. The [patch record](https://crimsonreporthub.com/patches) keeps official patch coverage and the archive together.

## Subscribe

Add either feed to your feed reader:

- [Atom](https://crimsonreporthub.com/feed.xml)
- [RSS](https://crimsonreporthub.com/rss.xml)

Both feeds contain original Hub articles and official patch rundowns. External coverage and player submissions stay out of them.

## Follow community work

[Issues](https://github.com/Statusnone420/Crimson-Desert-Report-Hub-Community/issues) track website problems, corrections, and suggestions. [Discussions](https://github.com/Statusnone420/Crimson-Desert-Report-Hub-Community/discussions) is the place for questions and conversation about the Hub. An open suggestion is not a commitment to a release date.
