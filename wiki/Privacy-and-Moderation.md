# Privacy and moderation

The Hub has no player accounts and does not add advertising or analytics trackers. The website's [Privacy page](https://crimsonreporthub.com/privacy) describes its data handling.

## On the website

Public pages show aggregate check-in counts, approved historical excerpts, published coverage, and public source context. Raw historical submissions, rejected source candidates, network hashes, and individual check-in records stay private.

Check-ins use a one-way network hash for duplicate prevention and rate limits. The application does not store raw IP addresses in its database. Shared networks can share one response, and a network change can count separately. This is why totals do not identify unique players.

Theme preferences and an optional Catch Up bookmark can be stored in your browser. These preferences are not sent to the server. Check-ins use Cloudflare Turnstile for abuse prevention.

## In the community repository

GitHub issues, discussions, and their attachments are public and require a GitHub account. They are separate from anonymous website check-ins. GitHub handles its own account data under its policies.

Keep passwords, account details, private messages, and personal logs out of posts. Check screenshots for personal information before uploading them.

## Moderation

Approved historical excerpts may be redacted. Editorial selections require review. Community discussion should stay respectful and relevant; harassment and spam may be removed.

For an incorrect detail or missing source credit, [send a correction](https://github.com/Statusnone420/Crimson-Desert-Report-Hub-Community/issues/new?template=correction.yml).
