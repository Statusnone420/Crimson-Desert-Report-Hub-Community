# Crimson Desert Report Hub

Welcome to the reader wiki for [Crimson Desert Report Hub](https://crimsonreporthub.com/), an independent fan newspaper for Crimson Desert.

- [Getting started](https://github.com/Statusnone420/Crimson-Desert-Report-Hub-Community/wiki/Getting-Started): find news, patches, videos, and check-ins.
- [How the Hub works](https://github.com/Statusnone420/Crimson-Desert-Report-Hub-Community/wiki/How-the-Hub-Works): understand sources and what the different counts mean.
- [Privacy and moderation](https://github.com/Statusnone420/Crimson-Desert-Report-Hub-Community/wiki/Privacy-and-Moderation): what is public and what stays private.
- [Reporting a problem](https://github.com/Statusnone420/Crimson-Desert-Report-Hub-Community/wiki/Reporting-a-Problem): choose the right place for a correction, website bug, or game issue.
- [Updates](https://github.com/Statusnone420/Crimson-Desert-Report-Hub-Community/wiki/Updates): follow reporting and patch coverage.

Use [Issues](https://github.com/Statusnone420/Crimson-Desert-Report-Hub-Community/issues/new/choose) for specific website problems and [Discussions](https://github.com/Statusnone420/Crimson-Desert-Report-Hub-Community/discussions) for questions and ideas.

The Hub is not affiliated with Pearl Abyss and cannot provide game or account support.
